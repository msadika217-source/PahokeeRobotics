FTC team 23812 Rising Phoenix is a dedicated robotics team located in Florida that designs, builds, and programs innovative robots while fostering creativity, teamwork, and problem-solving. We strive to grow our skills, support each other, and rise to every challenge.

Team Intel Profile

Our team, The Rising Phoenixes, is based at Pahokee Middle–Senior High School and consists of high school juniors passionate about robotics and innovation. Established in 2022, our team focuses on teamwork, creativity, and resilience while developing engineering and problem-solving skills. We strive to challenge ourselves, uplift others, and inspire the next generation of STEM leaders through robotics.

Engineering requires iteration. Many designs did not work the first time, but testing, analyzing results, and redesigning helped us improve our robot and critical problem-solving skills under pressure.

CORE ATTRIBUTES & MOTTO:

"Rise from the ashes to glory" — We focus on continuous improvement by learning new technical skills each season, and believe robotics is a platform for developing leadership, collaboration, and real-world engineering experience.

Mech Specifications: "Silas"

Our robot uses a modular design with a mecanum drivetrain that allows omnidirectional movement. Utilizing CAD to design the chassis and mechanisms completely beforehand, Silas has been engineered to perfectly complete this season's challenges.

ENGINEERING MODIFICATIONS & FEATURES:

• Turret-mounted dual flywheel shooter for faster aiming speed.
• Dual intake system for highly efficient artifact collection loops.
• Adjustable hood tracking for controlling shot trajectory adjustments.

⭐ SIGNATURE ABILITY: ADVANCED AUTONOMOUS CONTROLS

Features innovative and effective robot control systems, including advanced tracking sensors and precise autonomous execution that secured regional recognition.

Campaign Journey: DECODE

In this FTC season DECODE, our team focused on improving a wide variety of skills. Early in the season, we experimented with different robot designs, starting with a simple intake and shooter system. Through competitions and testing, we redesigned several mechanisms, including our shooter and chassis, using CAD and engineering principles. Along the way, we learned how to work better together, solve complex problems, and show others what is possible with robotics.

CAMPAIGN DIRECTIVES:

Strengthen team synergy; expand complex technical abilities; advance to higher levels of competition bracket logic; promote STEM education and host interactive robotics workshops.

UNIQUE BUFFS:

Extreme resilience during redesign loops, dual mentoring systems, and active bionic engineering design workflows (SignSync).
